  1. 1From the 1570s, meaning inferior in rank, and earlier, as a noun, a junior pupil.
  2. 2From Old French puisné, "born later, younger, youngest," the opposite of aisné, first-born.
  3. 3The sense "small, weak" comes in the 1590s.
  4. 4His twin puisne came to mean a junior judge.
